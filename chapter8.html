Kal raat bhi nau baje aaya, shayad aaj naaasake.Usne kamzor si awaaz mein kaha, "Main phone kar ke pooch leta hoon usse." Doctor Sibte Ali ne kaha, "Ji." Usne ba mushkil kaha..
